TODAY'S QUESTION

How does Ayushman Bharat help people get healthcare without facing heavy financial burdens?

KNOWLEDGE POINT 1

Protection From Hospital Bills

AB-PMJAY helps eligible families pay for major hospital treatment. Its cashless cover reduces the risk that serious illness will consume savings or push households into debt.

KNOWLEDGE POINT 2

Care Near Home

Ayushman Arogya Mandirs bring primary healthcare closer to communities. People can seek everyday care locally instead of depending only on large hospitals for health services.

KNOWLEDGE POINT 3

Stronger Public Facilities

PM-ABHIM supports hospitals, critical care and disease monitoring. Better health infrastructure strengthens the system that people depend on when serious illness or public health threats arise.

KNOWLEDGE POINT 4

Connected Health Records

The Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission helps patients securely share health records with their consent. Doctors at different facilities can access relevant information and provide more coordinated care.

KNOWLEDGE POINT 5

Care Across State Borders

AB-PMJAY allows eligible patients to receive cashless treatment at participating hospitals outside their home State. This helps migrant families and people seeking specialised care elsewhere.

QUICK FACTS


AB-PMJAY provides Rs 5 lakh per eligible family annually for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation. Unlike care centres, it covers hospital treatment; over 13 crore admissions were authorised.

Over 1.8 lakh Ayushman Arogya Mandirs offer nearby primary care. PM-ABHIM, launched in 2021 with Rs 64,180 crore, strengthens critical care and disease surveillance infrastructure.

The National Health Authority sets standards and IT platforms, while State Health Agencies manage hospital empanelment and claims. ABHA enables consent-based health record sharing.

Ayushman Vay Vandana covers people aged 70 and above regardless of income. Insurance coverage rose from 15% to over 50%; 97 crore ABHA numbers were reported.

KEY TAKEAWAY

Ayushman Bharat links hospital insurance, nearby primary care, stronger public facilities and digital health records, while cross-State treatment access helps reduce financial hardship and improve healthcare access.

MAINS QUESTION

What governance challenges must Ayushman Bharat address to deliver trustworthy healthcare across India?

MAINS ANSWER

Ayushman Bharat serves a large and diverse population. Its success depends not only on expanding coverage but also on maintaining reliable services and public trust.

The National Health Authority sets common standards and manages digital platforms, while State Health Agencies oversee hospitals and claims locally. This division supports local needs but requires strong coordination. Treatment packages must be updated so hospital payments remain realistic. Fraud checks, data analysis and action against hospitals that break rules are needed to protect patients and public funds. Digital claims systems can improve speed and transparency. The digital mission also creates responsibilities: patients must control the sharing of their records, and privacy needs protection. Verified provider directories, shared medical terminology and connected records can reduce fragmentation. Finally, service quality and access must improve as the scheme reaches older people, frontline workers and patients travelling between States.

The article therefore points to a continuing task: combine wider protection with accountable administration, safe health information and better care so coverage becomes meaningful for patients.

PRO MAINS ANSWER 1

Ayushman Bharat connects financial protection with local healthcare, public facilities and digital records to improve patient access.

What administrative and digital safeguards are needed for reliable Ayushman Bharat services?

PRO MAINS ANSWER 2

Reliable Ayushman Bharat services require coordinated administration, accountable hospitals and protection of patients' digital health information.

Hospital payment packages need periodic updates for realistic costs. Poor payments can weaken service quality. Fraud checks, claims analysis help safeguard patients and public funds. Services must also remain accessible to eligible patients seeking treatment across State borders. Expanded coverage for people aged 70 and above adds importance to dependable access. Governance must link national standards, local oversight and patient care.

Ayushman Bharat therefore needs both broader protection and stronger accountability. Effective coordination, realistic payments, trustworthy digital systems and consistent hospital services are essential to maintaining public confidence through effective public oversight.

TODAY'S QUESTION

How can India grow its economy while protecting the environment and using resources wisely?

KNOWLEDGE POINT 1

Cleaner Energy Sources

Solar and other renewable power can support growing energy needs while reducing dependence on more polluting sources. The article links this shift with new investment and manufacturing opportunities.

KNOWLEDGE POINT 2

Using Less Energy

Efficient lights, buildings and industries deliver useful services with less energy. This lowers waste and emissions while allowing homes and businesses to use resources more productively.

KNOWLEDGE POINT 3

Cleaner Industrial Production

Green hydrogen can help industries such as steel and fertilisers reduce emissions. Building this sector can also create work in equipment, renewable power, storage and transport.

KNOWLEDGE POINT 4

Saving Water And Materials

Water reuse, efficient irrigation and recycling reduce pressure on limited resources. A circular economy keeps materials useful longer instead of treating them only as waste.

KNOWLEDGE POINT 5

Resilient Cities And Communities

Better public transport, green buildings and urban wetlands can improve city life. Planning for floods, heat and water shortages also helps protect development from climate risks.

QUICK FACTS


The National Solar Mission began in 2010 to advance solar energy. The PLI scheme supports domestic manufacturing of high-efficiency solar photovoltaic modules.

PAT targets energy-intensive industries, while the Energy Conservation Building Code and Eco Niwas Samhita address buildings. Efficient lighting also helps reduce energy demand.

The National Green Hydrogen Mission targets 5 million metric tonnes annually by 2030, supported by approximately 125 GW renewable capacity and Rs 19,744 crore.

Extended Producer Responsibility addresses plastics and electronic waste, including recycling. Mission LiFE promotes responsible mobility, while adaptation includes early warnings, resilient agriculture and water security.

KEY TAKEAWAY

India can pursue green growth through renewable energy, efficiency, cleaner industry, recycling and climate-resilient cities, supporting economic development while reducing environmental harm.

MAINS QUESTION

What implementation challenges must India overcome to make green growth affordable and effective?

MAINS ANSWER

Green growth requires more than announcing environmental targets. The article stresses that policies must become practical, affordable solutions across industries, cities and communities.

Green hydrogen needs competitive production costs, buyers, infrastructure and clear standards before it can transform difficult industrial sectors. Water management requires treatment, reuse, groundwater recharge and local monitoring rather than only increasing supply. A circular economy needs markets for recycled materials, recovery facilities and stronger links with informal recyclers. Cities must coordinate transport, buildings, waste, energy and water systems. Climate adaptation also matters because heatwaves, floods and changing rainfall threaten livelihoods and infrastructure. Financing is another challenge: environmental goals need investment that supports innovation and long-term growth. Governments, businesses, financial institutions, researchers and citizens must coordinate their efforts, measure results and keep technologies accessible across regions and communities.

The article's central implementation lesson is to join finance, planning, monitoring and public participation. Green solutions will succeed more widely when they are affordable and useful in everyday development.
